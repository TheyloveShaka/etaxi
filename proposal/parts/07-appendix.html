Appendix AModel assumptions and method

Every cost, saving, emissions and fleet number in this document comes from one model, which compares energy cost only. This appendix reproduces its assumptions without change.

A.1 Assumptions

  1. Working days per year (312, six days a week) is an assumption, not a measured figure.
  2. Daily distance is derived, not measured: 35,000 km a year from an old minibus survey spread over 312 days gives 112 km a day. The conservative and favourable cases use 130 and 150 km.
  3. Fuel use is driver-reported litres per day from press interviews, treated as petrol.
  4. Electric energy use of 0.39 and 0.50 kWh per km comes from the low end and a midpoint of published projections; the Joylong E6 maker claim implies 0.29.
  5. Maintenance, insurance, driver pay, vehicle price and charger cost are excluded from the saving. Only energy is compared.
  6. Present values use an eight-year life (the battery warranty term published for one comparable van) and Uganda average lending rates for shilling and foreign-currency loans.
  7. Fleet phase sizes are planning targets set by this proposal, not forecasts.

A.2 Method in words

  • Petrol cost per day is litres used per day multiplied by the pump price per litre.
  • Electricity cost per day is distance per day, multiplied by kWh per kilometre, multiplied by the tariff per kWh.
  • Saving per day is petrol cost minus electricity cost; the percentage is that saving divided by petrol cost. Saving per year multiplies by 312 working days; the dollar figure divides by UGX 3,727 to the dollar.
  • Present value is the annual saving multiplied by the eight-year annuity factor at the stated lending rate.
  • Petrol CO2 is litres per year multiplied by 2.3 kg; grid CO2 is kWh per year multiplied by 0.137 kg; the difference is the CO2 avoided.
  • Fleet figures multiply the base-case per-vehicle figures by 30, 300 and 2,000 vehicles and by 14 seats. The pack cost of USD 9,299 is 86.1 kWh multiplied by USD 108 per kWh.
